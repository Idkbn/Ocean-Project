The Sunlight Zone

0 - 200m: Where most ocean life thrives.

Most floating plastic is found in this zone in massive patches.

The Twilight Zone

200 - 1,000m: Light becomes a memory.

Plastic here is in transit, it sinks slowly or is being eaten by migrating fish

The Midnight Zone

1,000 - 4,000m: Only bioluminescence exists here.

Large "Ghost" nets from fishing boats often sink and get snagged within this zone

The Abyssal Zone

4,000 - 6,000m: Freezing temperatures and crushing pressure.

Reasearch suggests that the seafloor is where nearly all ocean plastic ends up

The Hadal Zone

6,000m+: The deepest trenches on Earth.

Every a single crustacean tested in the Marianna trench had plastic in its gut :(
